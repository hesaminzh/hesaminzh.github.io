About

I hold an M.Sc. in Telecommunications Engineering from Shahid Beheshti University (National University of Iran). My research focuses on signal processing and optimization for wireless communications, particularly in reconfigurable intelligent surface (RIS), beyond-diagonal RIS (BD-RIS), and multi-antenna signal processing. I am passionate about developing and analyzing advanced methods to enhance the performance and efficiency of next-generation wireless systems.

Beyond-Diagonal RIS-aided Outdoor-to-Indoor mmWave Communications

H. Nezhadmohammad, R. Asvadi, and A. Pirhadi (2025). [PDF] [code]

Research focus. How should an outdoor base station and a reconfigurable surface be jointly optimized to serve indoor mmWave users when walls and blockages weaken their signals? We compare RIS connection architectures, optimization methods, and their effects on data rate, runtime, and outage.

Connection types: FC allows interconnections among all RIS elements; GC connects elements within separate groups; SC controls elements individually. WSR (weighted sum rate) is the total of the users' rates after applying their assigned weights; higher values indicate better aggregate performance.

Impact of Beyond-Diagonal RIS Inter-Panel Spacing in mmWave Communications

H. Nezhadmohammad, R. Asvadi, and A. Pirhadi (2026). [PDF] [code]

Research focus. How does the distance between RIS panels influence the achievable data rate in outdoor-to-indoor mmWave communication? We study this question across different RIS sizes and connection architectures, then optimize panel spacing together with the transmit and RIS configurations.

How to read the results. WSR (weighted sum rate) measures the combined users' data rates after weighting; higher is better. M is the number of RIS elements per panel. Stem-connected and tree-connected RIS allow structured interconnection patterns between elements, whereas single-connected RIS controls each element independently. The plots compare the proposed optimized-spacing design with a reference using fixed spacing.
